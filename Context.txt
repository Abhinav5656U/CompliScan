# Problem Statement: SIH26034
**Title:** Software System to check compliance of Packaged Commodities under Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011
**Sponsoring Ministry:** Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution
**Objective:** To develop a system that checks the compliance of packaged commodities with legal metrology rules by scanning products, images, and labels.

# Meet MeteroLens: The Complete AI Metrology Solution

We have built **MeteroLens**, acomprehensive, hybrid AI-powered platform designed to completely digitize and automate the work of Legal Metrology Officers. 

Here are the primary capabilities that make MeteroLens stand out:

1. **(USP) Intelligent Data Extraction with Smart Fallbacks:** Our system doesn't rely on just one engine. It uses **Gemini 2.5 Flash** for multimodal OCR and structured JSON field extraction, but seamlessly falls back to **PaddleOCR (Multilingual)** if needed, ensuring we never miss a detail on messy or curved packaging.

2. **(USP) Custom Model Training & Object Detection:** We didn't just use out-of-the-box APIs. We trained a custom **YOLOv8** object detection model combined with OpenCV to accurately detect bounding boxes of mandatory declarations, completely automating the visual inspection process.

3. **(USP) Physical Dimension Calibration:** We completely remove human subjectivity when measuring if a font size is legal (Rule 7).Our YOLOv8 model combined with OpenCV helps calculate the exact font size.

4. **(USP) Hybrid Compliance Rule Engine:** We use a two-pronged approach to check compliance: a blazing-fast Deterministic Engine (Regex and geometry checks) for strict rules, paired with a Semantic LLM Judge (Groq LLaMA 3) to evaluate ambiguous claims and ingredients.

5. **(USP) Automated Improvement Notice Generator (Jan Vishwas Act):** When a manufacturer makes a minor labeling mistake for the first time, our system checks their history and automatically drafts a legally formatted PDF "Improvement Notice," giving them a 30-day grace period to fix it—fully automating the government's new workflow!

6. **(USP) End-to-End Legal Reporting & Evidence Integrity:** Every single image captured is instantly locked with a **SHA-256 cryptographic hash**, ensuring the digital evidence is tamper-proof. The system then automatically generates court-admissible PDF compliance reports (compliant with Section 65B(4) of the Evidence Act) using ReportLab.

7. **(USP) Bilingual Semantic Consistency Checker:** The AI doesn't just read English; it reads Hindi (Devanagari) right alongside it. It cross-checks both languages to ensure companies aren't printing misleading translations or contradicting themselves on the package.

8. **(USP) Automatic Unit Sale Price (USP) Calculator:** No more mental math for inspectors. The system extracts the MRP and Net Quantity, calculates the exact price per gram/ml, and verifies that the label follows the strict LMPC rounding rules.

//9. **(USP) State Portal API Integrator (Vaidhmapan Sync):** We've eliminated double data entry. As soon as an officer finishes a digital inspection on MeteroLens, the compliance reports, fines, and GPS data are instantly synced into official state databases like Vaidhmapan in real-time.

10. **(USP) E-commerce SKU Crawler (Rule 18):** Non-compliance isn't just physical anymore. An officer can scan a physical product's barcode, and our system will automatically crawl its online listing (like Amazon or Flipkart) to ensure the digital label perfectly matches the physical rules.

11. **(USP) Bulk Multi-Scan & Panoramic Stitching:** Instead of taking a single photo, officers can rapid-fire scan multiple sides of a package or even a whole batch of different products. The AI intelligently stitches the angles together into a single panoramic view and processes everything simultaneously.

12. **(USP) GTIN/Barcode Risk Scoring & Dashboard Analytics:** A historical analytics engine tracks compliance failures by product GTIN and automatically assigns a Risk Tier (High/Medium/Low). This is displayed on a React-based web dashboard featuring trend graphs and geographic mapping of offenses.

13. **Citizen Crowdsourcing with GPS Tracking:** A dedicated public reporting portal allows everyday consumers to upload non-compliant products. The system automatically tags their GPS coordinates and flags the issue directly into the inspector's prioritized "Risk Queue".

14. **Interactive Multilingual AI Assistant:** An embedded conversational AI that can explain complex legal rule violations to citizens, draft formal complaint emails, and route them directly to the correct State Controller.

# Corrected Technology Stack for MeteroLens (Actual Implementation)

1. **Capture App & Frontend (User Interface)**
- Core Framework: React.js (v18) (Responsive Web App)
- Styling: Tailwind CSS
- Dashboard Analytics: Recharts (for trend graphs)
- Map Visualization: react-leaflet (for geographic plotting of scans)
- Web Scanner: html5-qrcode (for browser-based QR/Barcode scanning)
- 3D Interactive Canvas: React Three Fiber (@react-three/fiber, three.js)

2. **Backend Framework & Authentication**
- Core Framework: Flask (v3.0.3, Python) with modular Flask Blueprints
- Authentication: JWT-based Role Authentication (Flask-JWT-Extended, bcrypt)
- Serialization & Security: Marshmallow, Flask-Limiter, Flask-Talisman
- Asynchronous Task Queue: Celery (with gevent)

3. **Database & Storage**
- Relational Database: PostgreSQL hosted on Supabase (via psycopg2-binary) managed with Flask-SQLAlchemy, utilizing Supabase Connection Pooler (Session Mode) for IPv4 network compatibility.
- In-Memory Data Store / Caching: Redis
- Evidence Storage: Cloudinary API (for secure image hosting)

4. **AI Label Detection, OCR, & Object Detection (Hybrid Pipeline)**
- Primary AI Engine (Vision + NLP): Gemini 2.5 Flash. Handles multimodal label text extraction (OCR) and structured JSON field mapping simultaneously.
- Fallback OCR Engine: PaddleOCR (Multilingual English + Hindi) 
- Object Detection: YOLOv8 (via ultralytics) for precise geometrical validation.
- Image Pre-processing: OpenCV (cv2) + Pillow for denoising and automated image stitching.

5. **Compliance Rule Engine (Hybrid Deterministic + LLM)**
- Deterministic Validation: Python-based script executing Regex pattern matching and physical font-height bounds checking.
- LLM Judge (Semantic Verification): Groq API (qwen/qwen3.8-27b) evaluates ambiguous rules semantically.
- Bilingual/Script Compliance Detector: Groq API (llama3-70b-8192) cross-checks English vs. Hindi (Devanagari).

6. **Reporting & Evidence Non-Repudiation**
- PDF Generation: ReportLab (Python) for automated compliance certificate generation and Improvement Notices.
- Evidence Integrity: SHA-256 Cryptographic Hashing applied to image evidence at the time of capture.
